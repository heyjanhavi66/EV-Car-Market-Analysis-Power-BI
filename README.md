# 🚗 EV Car Market Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes the Electric Vehicle (EV) market.

The dashboard provides insights into EV brands, models, pricing, driving range, sales performance, market segments, and overall market trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the overall EV market.
- Compare different EV brands and models.
- Analyze EV pricing and market segments.
- Evaluate driving range and technical performance.
- Understand EV sales and market trends.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Power BI
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- DAX

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Market Overview

This page provides an overall view of the EV market using important KPIs such as:

- Total EV Models
- Total Brands
- Average Price
- Average Driving Range
- Total Annual Sales

### 2. Pricing & Segment Analysis

This analysis focuses on:

- EV price comparison
- Market segments
- Vehicle body types
- Brand comparison

### 3. Technical Performance Analysis

This page analyzes:

- Driving range
- Vehicle performance
- Technical specifications
- EV model comparison

### 4. Sales & Market Trends

This page focuses on:

- Annual sales performance
- Brand performance
- Market trends
- EV market growth

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- EV pricing varies across different brands and market segments.
- Driving range is an important factor in evaluating EV performance.
- Sales trends help identify high-performing brands and market opportunities.
- Interactive dashboards make it easier to explore EV market data.
